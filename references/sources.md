## brag (latent-spaces/brag, MIT)

https://github.com/latent-spaces/brag. Seven rules were folded in from it:

| Rule | brag file | Where it lives here |
|---|---|---|
| Reading time: label at least 0.8 s settled, sentence about 0.3 s per word, counted from when the whole line is on screen; fast in, then hold | `skills/brag/SKILL.md`, `references/step-2-plan.md` | `techniques.md` section 7; `critic.md` read time gate; `board-format.md` check 5 |
| Mid-transition stills before any full render; stagger or dip, never a plain crossfade between busy layouts | `skills/brag/SKILL.md` | `pipeline.md` section 5; SKILL.md step 5; `critic.md` seams gate |
| Poster baked into frame 0, same duration and frame count, audio copied | `references/step-4-deliver.md` | `scripts/poster.sh`; `pipeline.md` section 7; SKILL.md step 6 |
| 1 to 3 strong cue locks; up to 0.15 s for a major reveal, 0.10 s for a smaller entrance | `references/step-3-compose.md`, `references/audio.md` | `techniques.md` section 7; SKILL.md steps 2 and 3 |
| Effects in the music's key and space, soft under the music | `skills/brag/SKILL.md`, `references/audio.md` | `techniques.md` section 8 |
| Subtle audio-reactive treatment; no visualisers, strobing or text scaling | `references/audio.md`, `references/step-3-compose.md` | `techniques.md` section 8; `scripts/envelope.py` |
| WCAG contrast gates as an error; zero text checked is unverified, not a pass | `references/step-4-deliver.md` | `scripts/contrast.mjs`; `critic.md` contrast gate; SKILL.md step 6 |

brag also bundles music by ende.app (CC BY 4.0, Sascha Ende; credit "Music by ende.app") and 13 CC0 effects (8 by Kenney, 5 keypresses by unicae_games).
