# Taste library

The references this skill measures new films against. Step 0 adds the director's picks for each film to this list; at the end of a film, add what they called magic or average.

Each entry says how we know it:
- **Measured**: the file was downloaded and measured (cuts, loudness, frames).
- **Ours**: the author made it.
- **Read about**: only the maker's notes, prompt or a written study. Watch before you copy a move.

## 1. The main reference: Google, Gemini 4 Argon (Measured)

https://x.com/Google/status/2110041297360699856. 50.5 s, 24 fps.

- **Grammar:** real input on the left, the model's working output on the right, about 30 pairs. After the turn the film never explains again; it proves the claim 30 more times.
- **Hook:** a real hand opens a real notebook. By 2.5 s a sketch is dragged into the prompt bar. The mechanism is shown once, in the real UI, inside 3 s, with no title.
- **Turn:** 7.5 s, the real kite next to the simulated kite, on a music jump of about 12 dB.
- **Rhythm:** one move for 7 s, then a cut about every 1.0 s, with the two halves of the split cutting out of step (left, then right), which doubles the felt pace without hurting reading. A few full-frame shots let the eye rest. A 2-frame stop-motion burst at 44 s, a 2 s hold, black.
- **Words:** almost none. Only real interface text. The brand appears in the last 6 s, stamped on real walls.
- **Sound:** music only. About -29 dBFS for 6 s, a step to about -14 at the turn, a dip near 28 s, a fade to silence by 50 s.
- **Take:** input next to output; show the mechanism once then only results; end on a mark made from the world, not a slide.

## 2. Our own

### Film A, locked (Ours)

20.0 s, a product launch film about building an agent from one typed sentence. Opens on one line with three audiences named one per beat, then capability chips. The line glides into the real product window: a typed sentence, the product asks a question, the spec, a confirm, 5 tools picked inside the reasoning steps, three examples marked passed, a trial run, then a crowd of the product's mascot art.
- Every frame is HTML computed from `seek(t)`, with the real gradient, production option rows and real buttons. Score in code (`synth.py`, C major, 120 BPM). Zero generated video. About USD 0.05 of stills, all for a dropped direction.
- Measured with this skill's `check.py`: 1200 frames, 3840x2160, 60 fps, 20.000 s; -15.6 LUFS, -3.0 dBTP; one deliberate 0.15 s dip to black at 17 s before the end card. Beat grid of the score: 120.05 BPM, confidence 0.76.

### Film B, abandoned (Ours)

36 s, fully generated, following a craftsperson. Never finished: 19 cuts, about USD 78 of generation credit. Verdict: "not coherent at all". The counter-example for every rule in `learnings.md` section 9.

## 4. The Slopcore films (Read about: making-of notes only)

## 6. Glaido: take the type system, not the film (Measured)

https://x.com/glaido_AI/status/2110791247950758386. 94 s, about 75 cuts on the voice-over phrases.
- **Take:** word-level kinetic captions with exactly two emphasis styles (a blue highlight box for verbs and outcomes, a red condensed serif for the problem word); the brand colour held back until the product appears; "Every screen changed. The keyboard didn't."; a held black "Until now." before the reveal; an end card with mark, URL pill and one qualifier.
- **Do not take:** the 94 s length, the product arriving 63 s in, a flat music bed, and borrowed film clips you could not license.

## 8. Hype and average, said plainly

- **One-line "make a slick video" prompts**: viral for taking 1 minute and USD 2, not for craft. Template motion and generic copy.
- **OPUSCAR 98** (lemo, 98 styles in 6:31): a style swatch book, not a film.
- **The bottom of mg-styles' own jury** (7.57 to 7.64): competent genre exercises.
- **"Top 20 YouTube videos" as a teacher**: it gives you the median.
- **Brand-imitation films**: good brief structure, but built on someone else's trademark.
- **Film A's first cut, as shipped**: right bones, too little effort.
